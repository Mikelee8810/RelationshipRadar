# Relationship Rules

## Tracking

Every saved contact is tracked automatically.

Tracking and reminders are separate.

## What counts as contact

Counts:

- Outgoing calls, answered or unanswered
- Answered incoming calls
- Direct messages sent
- Voice notes
- Photos, links, stickers, GIFs, songs, locations
- Group chat messages to groups containing the person
- Manual in-person logs
- Scheduled or automated outgoing messages

Does not count:

- Reading messages
- Receiving messages without replying
- Likes, reactions, views

## People matching

- High confidence matches merge automatically.
- Uncertain matches ask once.
- User decisions control final merge state.

## Archived contacts

Deleting a phone contact archives the relationship.

History remains available.

If the person returns, restore and merge when possible.
